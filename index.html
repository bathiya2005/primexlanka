Sustainability

Leading the way to a sustainable future

At Primex Lanka Plastic, sustainability is not just a commitment—it’s a responsibility. As a leading polyethylene manufacturer, we prioritize minimizing environmental impact while delivering high-quality products.

Our closed-loop recycling system allows us to efficiently collect, process, and reuse production waste, reducing raw material consumption and plastic waste. Through eco-friendly technologies, we continuously innovate to create sustainable polyethylene solutions that meet industry demands responsibly.

At Primex Lanka Plastic, we believe progress and sustainability go hand in hand. By transforming waste into opportunity, we are shaping a cleaner, greener future.

  • Closed-loop recycling
  • Reused production waste
  • Eco-friendly technologies
